About CavelleYachts

A Yacht Brokerage Built on Trust, Discretion & Access

Abu Dhabi • Dubai • GCC • International

Cavelle specializes in yacht sales, acquisition and global sourcing. We help clients discover, evaluate and acquire the right yacht — whether for private living, investment, hospitality or a combination of all three.

Our Story

Built for the Way People Actually Use Yachts

CavelleYachts was founded on a simple observation: most people looking for a yacht don’t want a sales pitch — they want clarity, options and a partner who understands their actual goals.

Whether you are buying your first yacht, expanding an existing portfolio or exploring a hospitality concept on the water, our role is the same: to present suitable options, explain the trade-offs honestly and help you make a confident, informed decision.

We are a sales and sourcing brokerage. We do not manufacture, build or construct yachts. Our value lies in our network, our market knowledge and our ability to match the right vessel to the right purpose. Cavelle specializes in yacht sales, acquisition and global sourcing — from the UAE and GCC to markets across Europe, the Mediterranean and beyond.

Our Approach

Honest, Specific & Pressure-Free

We believe yacht buying should feel like a conversation, not a sales funnel.

That means we listen first. We ask about how you plan to use the yacht, where, and with whom — because those answers determine which options are actually worth your time.

We present real options with real trade-offs. We don’t overstate pricing potential, guarantee returns or promise uses that local regulations may not allow. Our recommendations are specific to your situation, and we’d rather tell you something isn’t a good fit than push a sale.

Important Note

What We Are — and What We Are Not

CavelleYachts is a yacht sales and sourcing brokerage. We do not manufacture, build or construct yachts and do not own a shipyard or manufacturing facility.

Yacht availability, specifications and pricing are confirmed directly by our sales team and are subject to change. Commercial, rental, hospitality and business uses of any yacht are subject to local regulations, marina requirements, permits and applicable law.

Nothing on this website constitutes financial, investment or legal advice, and no rental income or return is guaranteed. We encourage all clients to seek independent professional advice before making a purchase decision.
