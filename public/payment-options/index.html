Payment Options

Flexible, Verified Payment Methods

We accommodate a small set of trusted payment methods for qualifying transactions, always confirmed directly by our sales team.

Available Methods

How You Can Pay

The following payment methods are available for qualifying transactions.

C

Cash

Accepted for qualifying in-person transactions, confirmed directly with our sales team.

B

Bank Transfer

A standard method for yacht purchases, with official instructions confirmed directly by our team.

B

Bitcoin (BTC)

Accepted for qualifying transactions after buyer and transaction verification.

U

USDT

Accepted for qualifying transactions after buyer and transaction verification.

Important

Payment Details Are Never Automated

CavelleYachts does not operate automated payment processing, cryptocurrency checkout or published wallet or bank account details online.

Payment instructions — including bank details or wallet addresses — are provided directly by our sales team only after buyer and transaction verification. For large transactions, always confirm official payment instructions directly with our sales team before sending any funds.
