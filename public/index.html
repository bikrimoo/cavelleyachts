Depending on the asset, its configuration, location and applicable regulations, a Cavelle floating asset can support a wide range of lifestyle, hospitality and investment uses.

01

Private Residence

A private waterfront home offering privacy, space and panoramic views, subject to local marina and residency regulations.

02

Luxury Holiday Home

A seasonal retreat for family and guests, combining hospitality-grade interiors with life on the water.

03

Rental & Airbnb-Style Accommodation

04

Floating Restaurant / Café

Larger, hospitality-oriented layouts may suit dining or café concepts where zoning and marina approval allow.

05

Private Events & Entertainment

A striking, self-contained venue for private gatherings, celebrations and entertaining on the water.

06

Boutique Hospitality

A distinctive, design-led alternative to a conventional hotel or serviced-apartment concept.

07

Waterfront Business

A flexible base for a waterfront-facing commercial concept, subject to applicable licensing.

08

Investment Opportunity

A tangible, design-forward asset to consider alongside a wider property or lifestyle portfolio — with no guaranteed return.

Not every asset is suitable for every use. Commercial, rental and hospitality uses are subject to local regulations, marina requirements, permits and applicable law.
