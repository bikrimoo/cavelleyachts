About The Corniche

The Corniche is defined by its sculpted, architectural silhouette — sharp fin-like detailing, dark tinted windows and a low, confident profile on the water.

It has been captured both cruising against Abu Dhabi's skyline and at anchor in clear turquoise waters, demonstrating a design equally suited to city waterfronts and open coastal settings.

A yacht of this style suits buyers seeking a striking design statement for private cruising, waterfront living or as a distinctive addition to a wider portfolio.
